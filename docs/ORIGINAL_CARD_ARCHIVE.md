# Original Card Archive — Elemental Beast TCG (2024–2025)

This document preserves the exact historical record of cards identified and transcribed from authentic original physical/digital cards and creator recollections from the original 2024–2025 game development.

---

## Authenticity Categories

- **[CONFIRMED]**: Directly verified from authentic original cards. Stats, costs, abilities, and identities are preserved exactly without alteration.
- **[REMEMBERED]**: Explicitly recalled by the game's creator (e.g. 2-Mana Little Dragon, 5-Mana turn refresh).
- **[PROVISIONAL]**: Designed as reconstructed placeholders to enable complete gameplay while awaiting additional original card images.
